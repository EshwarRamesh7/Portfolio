01 / ABOUT

Curious by nature.
Careful with data.

I'm an aspiring Data Analyst building my career at the intersection of Economics, Data Science and practical business problem-solving. My thesis research and final presentation are complete, and I'm now looking to apply that learning to real teams and real decisions.

I enjoy the whole process: understanding a question, cleaning imperfect data, exploring patterns, building a model or dashboard, and then explaining the result in plain language.

I'm also developing a second lane in website design and development. That means I can help a small business not only understand its numbers, but also present itself professionally online.

I don't pretend to have years of industry experience. I focus instead on showing the work, explaining the thinking and building things carefully.
